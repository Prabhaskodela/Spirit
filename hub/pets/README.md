# Ask AI companions

The pixel animals in Ask AI come from [Menagerie](https://github.com/devkancheti4-design/menagerie), a chat UI where a
pixel animal does the typing. They are its exported sprite sheets (the Petdex format: 192 × 208 frames, 8 columns,
9 rows of moves), used here under the Apache License 2.0.

`pets.json` lists the animals and the moves. `../pet.js` plays them.
